This version improves the Body module:

- You can describe your plate before scanning it, and the result comes back separated by food, with each part editable.
- Liquids are now logged in milliliters.
- A scan no longer discards the whole analysis when a label comes back different from what was expected.
- Body fat percentage and somatotype only appear when they were actually estimated — the app no longer shows 0% in place of data nobody provided.
- Fixes to the day rollover and to sleep readings.
