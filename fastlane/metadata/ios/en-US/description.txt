Alma brings guided meditation, a place to log how you feel, and an AI companion together in a single app — at your own pace.

"Take care of your soul" is the starting point: short breathing and mindfulness practices, so you can build a habit that fits your routine.

Note: Alma's spoken meditations and in-app content are in Brazilian Portuguese.

--------------------

FREE, NO SUBSCRIPTION

- The first 3 days of the guided meditation program
- The "Água Corrente" (Running Water) sound, to relax and focus
- Corrente de Paz: keep track of your practice streak
- Mood check-in: log how you feel, every day
- Corpo (Body): log your meals, water, weight and supplements, and run the workouts from the catalog — your diary is yours, and it costs nothing
- Feed: a selection of links and videos published by our team

WITH ALMA PREMIUM

Guided meditation
The full program: 30 practices, 5 to 15 minutes each, organized into four stages — Breathing, Body Awareness, Emotions and Integration. Plus 7 more sounds and music tracks to relax, focus and sleep.

Talk to your Alma
The conversation remembers what you have already shared. Every message starts with context, not from scratch.

Your analyses
Your mood trends across the weeks, your history, and the reflection of the day.

Your health in the app
Steps, sleep and heart rate read from the Health app, if you allow it.

Women's Health and Liberdade
Follow your cycle, with predictions for your period, ovulation and fertile window. And keep track of your days away from a habit you want to leave behind.

--------------------

THE BODY MODULE, INSIDE THE SAME APP

Alma includes a complete body module — no separate app to download, no separate subscription.

Free: log meals, water, weight and supplements; run the workouts from the catalog and see the detail of each exercise.

With Premium: scan your meal or your body from a photo and get the reading right away; build your own workout; open the muscle map and a library of more than a thousand exercises; follow your history and your analyses.

The photo is analyzed and is not stored by Alma. The AI provider may retain it for up to 30 days for safety purposes only, and does not use it to train models.

--------------------

PRIVACY FIRST

Mood, cycle and habits are stored only on your device, in encrypted storage. This data is not written to our servers and is not sent to analytics or advertising tools.

Alma does not track you across apps and does not use advertising identifiers.

If you allow it, and only then, a short summary of your day travels with your conversation so Alma can understand you better. You can delete your account and your data from within the app.
